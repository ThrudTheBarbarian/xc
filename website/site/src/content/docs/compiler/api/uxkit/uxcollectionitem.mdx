`UXCollectionItem` is one tile of a
[`UXCollectionView`](/compiler/api/uxkit/uxcollectionview/): the `obj`
you passed to `addItem` (returned untouched at selection time), the
`label` drawn beneath the tile, and the laid-out frame (`x`, `y`, `w`,
`h`) that the grid's `layout` writes and its hit test reads.

You rarely construct one yourself; `addItem(obj, label)` does. The laid-out
frame is public so that tests can check the grid's arithmetic by reading these
fields.

## Fields

- `obj`: your object, whatever the grid is showing.
- `label`: the text under the tile.
- `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`: the tile's laid-out frame, written by `layout`.

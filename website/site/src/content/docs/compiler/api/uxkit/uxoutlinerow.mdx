---
title: UXOutlineRow
description: "A table row that knows its outline: it draws the disclosure triangle in the indent gutter and toggles its node on a click there."
---

`UXOutlineRow` is the [`UXTableRow`](/compiler/api/uxkit/uxtablerow/)
subclass an [`UXOutlineView`](/compiler/api/uxkit/uxoutlineview/) makes
for its lines. It draws the disclosure triangle in the indent gutter
(pointing right when closed, down when open). A click inside the
triangle's box flips the node's expansion and reloads. Any other click
is an ordinary row click, handled by the table.

The view's `newRow` hook constructs these; nothing else does.

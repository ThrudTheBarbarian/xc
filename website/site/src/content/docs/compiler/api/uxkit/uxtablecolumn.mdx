---
title: UXTableColumn
description: "One column of a UXTableView: a title and a width, and nothing else."
---

`UXTableColumn` is one column of a
[`UXTableView`](/compiler/api/uxkit/uxtableview/): a `title` and a
`width`, and nothing else. The table lays cells out from these two values,
and the data source supplies the content for each `(row, col)`.
`addColumn(title, width)` makes them.

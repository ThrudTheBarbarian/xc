This agreement is between you and Cognostem Ltd ("the Maintainer"), which
runs the xc project ("the Project"). It covers every
Contribution you submit to the Project, now or later. By signing it you accept
its terms for all of them.

You keep the copyright in your Contributions. This agreement grants licences;
it does not transfer ownership.

## 1. Definitions

**"You"** means the person signing this agreement. If you contribute as part
of your job, your employer may own your work instead; see section 5.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including changes
or additions to existing work, that you intentionally submit to the Project
for inclusion: by pull request, patch, issue, email or any other way, in
writing or electronically. Anything you mark in writing as "Not a
Contribution" is excluded.

This licence is granted to the Maintainer only. Everyone else receives your
Contributions under the open-source licence of the tree they went into, as
part of the Project, and may use and distribute them only on that licence's
terms.

You grant the Maintainer, and anyone who receives software distributed by the
Project, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge, royalty-free,
irrevocable patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell,

alleging that your Contribution, or the work it was submitted to, infringes a
patent, any patent licence granted to that party under this agreement for
that Contribution ends on the date the litigation is filed.

## 4. Commercial licences, and the Project's commitment

The Maintainer may also offer the Project, including your Contributions,
under separate commercial licences, for example to a company that wants to
distribute it on terms other than the open-source licence.

In return, the Maintainer agrees that every Contribution included in a
release will always remain available to everyone under the open-source
licence of the tree it went into, or another licence approved by the Open
Source Initiative. A commercial licence is offered alongside the open-source
one, never instead of it.

## 5. What you confirm

- You are legally entitled to grant these licences.
- If your employer has rights in what you create, you have permission from
  them to make your Contributions on its behalf, or your employer has waived
  those rights, or has signed a corporate CLA with the Project.
- Each Contribution is your original creation, apart from any third-party
  material you identify (see section 7).
- To your knowledge, your Contributions include no third-party material that
  you have not identified, and no patent or licence restriction you have not
  disclosed.
- If you used AI tools to produce part of a Contribution, you have reviewed
  that part and have the right to submit it under this agreement.

You do not have to provide support for your Contributions. Unless you agree
in writing, or the law requires it, your Contributions are provided "as is",
without warranties or conditions of any kind, including title,
non-infringement, merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 7. Work that is not yours

If you want to submit work that is not your original creation, submit it
separately, and say in the submission where it comes from, its licence, and
any restrictions you know of, marked as "Submitted on behalf of a third party:
[name]".

## 8. Keeping this accurate

You agree to tell the Maintainer if you learn that anything you confirmed in
this agreement has become untrue.
